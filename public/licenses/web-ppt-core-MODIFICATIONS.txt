Local modification notice for @web-ppt/core 0.4.5 (MIT)

2026-10-14: naver-ppt applies a build-time transformation to the pinned parser
to resolve background style references according to the OOXML style matrix:
0/1000 means no background, 1..999 selects fillStyleLst, 1001 and above
selects bgFillStyleLst starting at its first entry. Invalid references fail
instead of being clamped to the last background style.
The parser's per-slide fallback rethrows this specific unresolved-reference
error, so a white placeholder cannot be returned as successful parsing.

The original copyright and MIT license remain in web-ppt-core-LICENSE.
The source transformation and its pinned input hash are maintained in
scripts/pptx-background-patch.ts. Source presentations are not modified.
